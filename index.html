The Networked Systems Group (NSG) conducts research at the intersection of networking, distributed systems, and artificial intelligence. We build intelligent infrastructure across cloud, edge, and space.

Research Focus
Networked AI Systems
Exploring the convergence of AI and networking, including AI for networks and networks for AI.
Programmable and AI-Native Infrastructure
Advancing programmable, heterogeneous, and AI-native hardware and system architectures.
Cloud and Distributed Infrastructure
Designing efficient, resilient, and scalable infrastructures for cloud-scale services and applications.
Space Computing and Networking
Building integrated networking and computing architectures for future space-cloud systems.

Our Symbol

The infinity symbol represents our belief that “The net is vast and infinite.” Its green color conveys vitality and growth, recalling Goethe’s words: “Grau, teurer Freund, ist alle Theorie, und grün des Lebens goldner Baum.” It also pays tribute to Jesus College, Oxford, where this academic journey began.

The colors of NSG trace the experiences that shaped our research philosophy: blue for Oxford and Yale, where our view of systems research was formed; orange for Alibaba Cloud, where practical knowledge of networking, especially cloud networking, was cultivated; and purple for Nanjing University, whose support made it possible to establish the Networked Systems Group.
